ED-HRIS Compensation Management System

Timeline: Mar – Jun 2026  |  Live Prototype: ornate-phoenix-e37706.netlify.app

Overview

A compensation management system designed for a client experiencing documented pay disparities across race and gender lines, built as part of INFO 380: Product and Information Systems Management. As part of a 4-person team, I helped interview 7 stakeholders — HR managers, legal, VPs, and payroll — and translated their input into user stories, workflow diagrams, and a full requirements specification for a system built around compliance reporting, employee-facing pay transparency, and leadership analytics dashboards. The live prototype is framed around a pay equity audit trail — giving HR and leadership a clear, traceable record of how compensation decisions are made and reviewed.

What I did
Interviewed 7 stakeholders (HR, legal, VPs, payroll) as part of a 4-person team to scope the system
Translated stakeholder input into 5 user stories, workflow diagrams, and data flow diagrams
Defined acceptance criteria and authored the full requirements specification
Designed the system around 3 pillars: compliance reporting, employee-facing pay transparency, and leadership analytics dashboards
Presented the final deliverable — requirements spec, prototype, and presentation — to the class/client
